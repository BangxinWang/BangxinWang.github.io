About Me

Since October 2024, I have been a PhD student in mathematics at Universität Hamburg, supervised by Ingo Runkel and Azat Gainutdinov. My research is funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG) under the Collaborative Research Center SFB 1624, Higher structures, moduli spaces and integrability. Prior to my doctoral studies, I obtained my Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering and Master's degree in mathematics at ETH Zürich. Originally, I grew up in Wuhan, China.

My research lies at the intersection of topological field theories, higher category theory, and representation theory. I am particularly interested in defects, background fields, and gauging in (non-semisimple) TFTs, and their associated algebraic structures.

AI Disclaimer

I frequently use AI in my workflow for learning, searching literature, discussing ideas, and polishing drafts. However, no AI-generated text is contained in my published papers. While I strongly support thoughtful discussions on how AI can serve the long-term interests of the mathematical community, I am deeply concerned by the current marketing-driven practices of AI companies. The commercialisation of AI tools has led to behaviours that can be considered research misconduct, and it frequently misrepresents the nature of mathematical research to the public.

This commercial encroachment is increasingly infiltrating our community. Events like Caltech's 40-hour, multimillion-dollar-sponsored Mathathon incentivise a rushed, unverified, and superficial approach to mathematics. Promoting this irresponsible, storytelling-style methodology stands in direct opposition to the values and traditions that our community has long revered and upheld. To protect these core principles, I have signed the open letter protesting this event.
